# SMRITI — Pattern Recognition Demo & Verification Guide
## Phase 03: Playable Sequence Recall, Local Persistence & Deterministic Adaptive Engine

---

### 1. Overview
**Pattern Recognition** is the second core cognitive exercise in SMRITI. Designed specifically for elderly individuals in the North Eastern Region (NER), it presents calm visual rhythms of culturally familiar items (Apples, Garden Orchids, Assam Tea Cups, Warm Sun, Bamboo Leaves, Family Homes, Festival Drums, River Boats) and invites the patient to choose which picture completes the sequence.

---

### 2. Core Game Rules & Formulas

#### 2.1 Difficulty Tiers
- **Easy (Level 1):** 4 questions, 3–4 visible items per sequence, 2 choices (1 correct, 1 distractor).
- **Medium (Level 2):** 5 questions, 4–5 visible items per sequence, 3 choices (1 correct, 2 distractors).
- **Hard (Level 3):** 6 questions, 5–7 visible items per sequence, 4 choices (1 correct, 3 distractors).

#### 2.2 Mathematical Formulas
- **Accuracy:**
  $$\text{accuracy} = \frac{\text{correctAnswers}}{\max(1, \text{totalAttempts})}$$
- **Scoring:**
  $$\text{score} = \max(0, (\text{correctAnswers} \times 100) - (\text{mistakes} \times 20) - (\text{hintsUsed} \times 10))$$
- **Adaptive Difficulty Thresholds:**
  - $\text{Accuracy} \ge 85\% \land \text{mistakes} \le 1 \land \text{hintsUsed} == 0 \implies \textbf{Increase Difficulty}$ (Level Up)
  - $\text{Accuracy} < 60\% \lor \text{mistakes} \ge 3 \implies \textbf{Support Trigger}$ (Level Down / Gentle Guidance)
  - Otherwise $\implies \textbf{Maintain Level}$

### 3. Step-by-Step Gameplay Demonstration

#### Step 1: Access the Exercise
1. Launch the SMRITI app in mobile or web preview.
2. Tap the **Games** icon in the bottom navigation bar.
3. Select the **Pattern Recognition** card ("Follow friendly visual rhythms").
4. Review the gentle "How to Play" instructions.
5. Select your difficulty level (**Easy**, **Medium**, or **Challenging**).
6. Tap **Start Pattern Exercise**.

#### Step 2: Interactive Gameplay
1. Observe the horizontal pattern sequence cards (e.g., `[Red Apple] -> [Garden Flower] -> [Red Apple] -> [Next ?]`).
2. Notice the large $\ge 64\text{dp}$ answer buttons at the bottom.
3. Tap **Hint** to eliminate one incorrect option (highlighted in subtle gray with "Eliminated").
4. Tap the correct answer card:
   - The empty `Next ?` slot immediately reveals the matching item with restorative sage highlight.
   - A calm notification banner displays: *"Well done! That fits the pattern."*
   - The exercise pauses for 1.4 seconds before transitioning smoothly to the next question.
5. In case of an incorrect selection:
   - A gentle prompt appears: *"Let’s look closely at the pattern again."*
   - The mistaken choice is dimmed so the user can comfortably pick another option without penalty panic.

#### Step 3: Results & Persistence Verification
1. Upon completing all questions, the **Exercise Completed** screen appears.
2. Metrics displayed:
   - **Accuracy percentage** (e.g. `100%`)
   - **Transparent Score** (e.g. `400`)
   - **Time Taken** (e.g. `35s`)
   - **Hints Used**
3. Verify the sync confirmation: **"Saved to Local Activity History"**.
4. Check the **Next Activity Suggestion** generated by the client adaptive engine (e.g., *"Wonderful focus! You followed every rhythm smoothly. The next exercise can include a slightly more varied activity level."*).
5. Tap **Return to Home** or **Back to Games**.
6. Switch to the **Progress** tab (Activity History):
   - Notice the new session: **Pattern Recognition (Level 1 - Easy)** with accuracy and completion timestamp listed in real-time.

### 5. Ethical & Clinical Boundaries
- SMRITI makes **zero clinical conclusions**.
- All performance data is stored as non-clinical engagement indicators (`accuracy`, `score`, `responseTimeMs`).
- Prohibited clinical terms (`"dementia"`, `"decline"`, `"diagnosis"`, `"impairment"`) are strictly banned by automated unit tests.
